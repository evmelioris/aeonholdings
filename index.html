About Evan

An operator looking for one company to call home.

Evan Melioris spent his career in operations, strategy, and applied AI and cloud technology at Accenture and Deloitte, working closely with organizations across healthcare and insurance. He holds an MBA in Finance from Emory University's Goizueta Business School.

He is now setting out to do one thing: acquire a single established business and operate it himself for the long haul. The goal is not a quick turnaround or a resale, but to be a hands-on owner who brings operational rigor and modern technology to a company that already has a strong foundation — while protecting what makes it work today.

Aeon Holdings is early by design. It is one person at the start of a focused, patient search, offering an owner something rare: a buyer who plans to stay.

What Aeon looks for

The kind of business worth stewarding for decades.

There is no single industry and no rigid checklist. Aeon is drawn to solid, lasting companies — the sort an owner has spent years building carefully. A few traits tend to matter most.

◆

Established & profitable

A durable company with a real history of steady earnings, not a turnaround or an experiment.

◆

Durable, recurring revenue

Often B2B services or operations-driven work, with loyal customers and revenue that holds up over time.

◆

A committed team

Capable people who know the business well and would thrive with continuity and support.

◆

A well-run foundation

Sound day-to-day operations and a good reputation with the customers and community it serves.

◆

An owner planning a transition

A founder thinking about retirement or succession who cares where the company goes next.

◆

Room to keep improving

A strong base where thoughtful operations and modern tools can help the next chapter, without disruption.

If you have spent years building something solid, this may well be about your company — wherever it sits, and whatever it does.

Our approach

Why an owner might choose Aeon.

Selling the company you built is a personal decision as much as a financial one. Aeon is designed around that reality.

Ownership, not a resale

The intent is to buy and hold for many years and operate the business directly — not to flip it, strip it, or pass it along to the next buyer.

Respect for your people & reputation

The team, the customers, and the name you built are the value. Preserving that legacy and continuity comes first.

Confidential & straightforward

Every conversation is private and low-pressure. The process is honest and clear, moving at a pace that respects you and your business.

Hands-on involvement

Aeon means an owner who shows up and runs the business — bringing operational discipline and modern technology to a strong foundation.
